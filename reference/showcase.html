A cozy game where physics is the rulebook

High school students explore a shared world with their class, form small groups, and learn physics by predicting what will happen, testing it, and comparing. Nothing here is final: it's an early look at the characters, how they move, and a small arena you can play in.

Make your bean

Every student plays as a bean they design themselves: pick a body form, a color and pattern, a hat and a face, then earn accessories by exploring. Cosmetics never change the physics.

Animations

Every student plays as a customizable bean. These loops show the orange bean, built from separate parts that are animated in code.

Turnaround

Five drawn views; the side and ¾ views are mirrored for the other directions.

Reactions

Little moments tied to gameplay: thinking during a prediction, Eureka! when it's right, Oops when it misses.

Walk and run

Side view bobs and steps; front and back views waddle. Running adds a lean, bigger strides and dust.

Jump

Crouch, launch, up, fall and land, with squash and stretch.

Push

The heavier the cart, the harder the bean strains. Let go and the cart keeps rolling.
