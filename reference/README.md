# Reference pages

Standalone HTML files; open them in a browser. They need internet for Google Fonts, and
`bean-turn.html` loads Three.js from cdnjs. **Behavior and look reference only. Never port their
code** (see `docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md`).

- `showcase.html`: the showcase draft. Sections: customization kit, animation strips, the playable
  arena (bench and Priya, carts, catapult with crank, swimmable pond with a shark fin, gate to the
  U-Track energy ride), the Field Station hub, the world map, and the Heavy Baron sheet. Every
  strip has a full-screen button. Its Baron drawings include the sash and mouth fix.
- `bean-turn.html`: four ways to turn the bean (drawn views, paper 3D, pre-rendered 3D, live 3D)
  driven by one dial, to compare how each handles rotation.
